John Wall's Career

John Wall was drafted out of Kentucy first overall to the Washington Wizards.

Wall started his career next to star guard Gilbert Arenas. After Arenas was traded, Wall took over the franchise point guard spot and became a legend in Washington.

He would play for the wizards from the 2010-11 NBA season until the 2018-19 season. He never averaged less then 16.3 points per game (PPG) and 7.6 assists per game (APG).

Wall was a five time All-Star and recieved third team All-NBA honors in 2017. He also made the All-Rookie team and All-Defensive second team in his time in Washington.

His career was altered after . Shortly after that, the Wizards traded him in a package for Russell Westbrook.

Wall would play a season in Houston and a season in Los Angeles with the Clippers before retiring from the NBA.
